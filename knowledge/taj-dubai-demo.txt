ROOMS
The hotel has four room types: the Deluxe Room (up to 2 guests), the Premier Room (up to 3 guests), the Luxury Suite (up to 4 guests) and the Presidential Suite (up to 4 guests).
Rates and availability depend on the dates and come from the booking system. Always check availability before quoting a price.
All rates are per room per night, in UAE dirhams (AED), and do not include taxes and fees, which are added at the hotel.

CHECK-IN AND CHECK-OUT
Check-in is from 3 pm. Check-out is by 12 noon.
Early check-in and late check-out can be requested and depend on availability on the day. They may carry an extra charge.

BREAKFAST
Breakfast is served daily from 6:30 am to 10:30 am. It is not included in the room rate unless the booking says so, and can be added at the hotel.

PARKING
Guest parking and valet parking are available at the hotel.

POOL, SPA AND GYM
The hotel has an outdoor pool, a spa and a fitness centre for guests. The pool and fitness centre are open daily from 6 am to 10 pm. Spa treatments are booked directly with the spa during the stay.

AIRPORT TRANSFERS
Airport transfers can be arranged on request, at an extra charge. Guests can mention this as a special request when booking.

CHILDREN AND EXTRA BEDS
Children are welcome. Children count towards the maximum number of guests for a room.
A baby cot can be provided free of charge on request. Extra beds are available in some room types on request and at an extra charge.

PETS
Pets are not allowed, except assistance animals.

SMOKING
All rooms are non-smoking. Smoking is only allowed in the designated outdoor areas.

CANCELLATION
Bookings can be cancelled free of charge until 48 hours before the arrival date. Cancellations after that, and no-shows, may be charged one night's rate.
To change a booking, the existing booking is cancelled and a new one is made, subject to availability.

PAYMENT
No payment is taken over the phone. Payment is made at the hotel at check-in or check-out, by card or cash.
